## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Category-interchangeable. Remove the word "Mistr" and this could be a video scrubber or a crypto chart: blue-black glass with backdrop blur, a cobalt→violet→pink gradient hairline around both bars (styles.css:79-102), stock outline icons, a round cyan play button, tracked uppercase micro-labels. Nothing in the chrome is about radar — no site ID, no minutes-ago timeline, no dBZ swatch. The "Stormlight" rose/violet/dawn hues are also the 60–70 dBZ colors of Mistr's own reflectivity ramp (palette.ts:40-42), and every dBZ value is set in rose `#FF7BAE` (styles.css:602), so a drizzle reading shows in hail-core pink — breaking the design's own Weather Separation Rule. The approved comp (docs/design/comps/mistr-alpha-v2-a-quiet-radar.webp) was more specific than what ships: it had `KTLX` in the toolbar and `-20 … NOW` on the timeline. Each "quieter" pass since removed product information and kept the generic styling. The engineering underneath is highly specific; the visual layer expresses none of it.

**Deterministic scan:** The CLI detector found 0 issues in RadarChrome.tsx and App.tsx, but it only regex-scans TSX markup; all styling lives in styles.css, which it can't scan. The live overlay (run via a workaround because the app's CSP blocks the detector's WebAssembly) flagged: `ai-color-palette` "cyan neon text on dark" on the scan clock (styles.css:453) and the play button (styles.css:470); `dark-glow` zero-offset cyan glow, most likely the timeline thumb (styles.css:508); `repeating-stripes-gradient` (advisory) on the timeline ticks (styles.css:534). The detector agrees with the review on the generic cyan-glow-on-glass look. Its "decorative stripes" flag looks like a false positive (they're frame ticks) but is half right: the ticks are drawn for 20 frames on a 60-frame loop, so they don't mean anything. Contrast checks did not run.

**Visual overlays:** None left in the app; the overlay was drawn once for a screenshot, then removed.

## Overall Impression

The bones are good and the look is borrowed. Layout discipline (two stable zones, fixed-geometry telemetry, strong keyboard plumbing) is well above average. But the chrome says almost nothing about radar, hides the single most important fact (which radar is on screen), makes the most common action (pick a site) slow and easy to fumble, and dresses it all in generic glass-and-gradient styling that steals the radar's own colors. Biggest opportunity: make the chrome state radar truth plainly — source, time + age together, a real loop span — and drop the decoration.

## What's Working

1. **Nothing jitters.** The telemetry cell is fixed-width with `contain: layout`, AM/PM width is reserved, and figures are tabular, so the bar never shifts while values refresh.
2. **Accessibility plumbing.** Roving tabindex with arrows/Home/End, focus moves into panels and returns to the trigger, forced-colors and reduced-motion handled, rich `aria-valuetext` on the timeline.
3. **Restraint.** Two content-sized zones, the map stays in charge, and the bar holds up at 1100×700.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] You can't see which radar is on screen.**
- Why: The chrome never shows National vs. a Site; it's only in a tooltip and aria-label (RadarChrome.tsx:208, 227). Zoom handoff swaps Level II and MRMS with no visible change. Even the critique's own captures were mislabeled because of this.
- Fix: Put the source in the toolbar's text slot (`KTLX` / `NATIONAL`, with an inline pending state `KTLX → KFWS`); demote or drop the wordmark.
- Command: /impeccable clarify

**[P1] Picking a site is slow, and the reflex keystroke is destructive.**
- Why: With National showing, the site panel opens on a National/Site toggle and a paragraph; search only appears after clicking Site (RadarChrome.tsx:635). Focus lands on National, so Enter flies the map to CONUS (App.tsx:4151-4159). DESIGN.md says the panel opens focused on search.
- Fix: Always open on the search field; make National the first row of the same list; drop the toggle and paragraph; Enter picks the top match, ↑/↓ move, a Recent group shows the last 3–5 sites; 36px rows.
- Command: /impeccable distill

**[P1] The playback bar makes you decode time.**
- Why: The age `01:38` has no unit and sits ~500px from the timestamp it describes; `60 / 60` is a frame index; ticks are drawn for 20 frames on a 60-frame loop (styles.css:534); the track sits ~7px above the bar's centerline; the loop span is never shown; the "loading recent" label is dead code (RadarChrome.tsx:347).
- Fix: `10:48:10 PM CDT · 1m 38s ago` together on the left; center the track; replace `60 / 60` with `−1h 58m … now`; ticks from the real frame count (or none); right cell for dBZ only; 15–16px for time/age/dBZ.
- Command: /impeccable layout

**[P1] Problems are nearly silent.**
- Why: Every notice is an 8px hover-only dot (below the 24px target minimum). A first-paint failure shows no cause or remedy to sighted users. The age stays green for 10 minutes after the newest scan (radarChromeModel.ts:219). Hint, pending, and settled inspection states look identical.
- Fix (no banners): the age cell itself changes state — amber plus a short word (`RETRYING`, `LOADING 12/60`); a 12px mark with a 24px hit area; show the real cause and remedy in the failure capsule; settled values in Day White; green threshold based on scan cadence.
- Command: /impeccable harden

**[P2] Generic look that collides with the radar colors.**
- Why: Glass + blur + full gradient ring (reads lopsided: cobalt left, grey right), cyan neon glow, pink dBZ values, stock icons (the recenter crosshair is the same glyph as the inspection reticle), a placeholder wordmark.
- Fix: Make the chrome achromatic (white/graphite) so color means data only; show dBZ with a swatch in the value's real palette color; replace the ring with one edge light or nothing; purpose-built glyphs for source, recenter, and view (showing the current mode).
- Command: /impeccable colorize, then /impeccable typeset

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user):** No Space for play/pause, no ←/→ without focusing the slider, no `/` or Ctrl+K for search. Switching sites from National takes 5+ actions. Enter in search does nothing. No recents among 155 sites. Esc doesn't clear the reticle.

**Sam (accessibility):** The status mark is an 8×8 focusable target. The source radio group has no arrow keys and both radios are tab stops. `aria-checked` and the check icon can disagree. 10px descriptor text; 11px date and zone. The wordmark's `aria-label` on a plain `<span>` is ignored. The live region reads `PAUSED · NEWEST` in caps. Attribution text is ~3:1. At 200% zoom, `min-width: 720px` + `overflow: hidden` can clip chrome.

**The owner (storm enthusiast, long live sessions):** Can't see Level II vs. MRMS while zoom handoff flips between them. Clear air in National says `DATA MISSING`. Every dBZ value is hail-core pink. The sampled value is far from the reticle, so the eyes ping-pong. The loop span is unknown. The age stays green for 10 minutes. A feed failure is an 8px dot. Map labels show through the glass behind the telemetry.

## Minor Observations

- The fonts don't match the spec: Barlow Condensed (the Label role) is never loaded (main.tsx:3-6), and Recursive is loaded with only the weight axis, so it isn't monospaced — which forced the AM/PM width hack (RadarChrome.tsx:716-728).
- Two sans voices: condensed Barlow up top, wide Recursive caps in the bar.
- Radii off the token scale: 7 (styles.css:190), 9 (:148, :359), 10 (:282); the 9px tool button inside the 12px bar gives non-concentric corners.
- Spacing off the 4/8/12/16/24 scale (`1px 5px`, `4px 9px`, `7px 14px`, 11px gaps).
- Icon strokes of 1.55 / 1.8 / 1.5 are inconsistent and blur at DPR 1.
- The 7px loading ring floats off the pin like a stray speck (styles.css:169-179).
- The reticle's crosshair covers the exact gate it samples; use a gapped crosshair.
- The play button is 34px, below the 40px control minimum in DESIGN.md.
- The date is shown on every frame; it only matters when the scan isn't from today.
- "CONUS" appears three times in the small source panel (RadarChrome.tsx:609, 621, 674).
- Preparation labels are engine stage names in caps (`READYING DISPLAY`, radarChromeModel.ts:174-180).
- Needs checking: whether National's `DATA MISSING` over clear air is really "no echo" (MRMS sentinel semantics, sampling.ts:24).
